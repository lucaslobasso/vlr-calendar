# Privacy Policy

VLR Calendar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- It runs only on vlr.gg pages, reading match information already shown on the page.
- When you hover or click the calendar icon, it fetches the relevant vlr.gg match, event and team pages (same site) to read the match details.
- It then opens calendar.google.com in a new tab, with the match details (teams, event, time, stream links, VLR link) in the URL. Whatever happens next is between you and Google.
- It uses no storage, cookies, analytics, tracking, or remote code, and sends nothing to the developer or any third party.

Contact: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
